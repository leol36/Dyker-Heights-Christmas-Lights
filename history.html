History Behind the Lights

How one family's tradition lit up an entire neighborhood

The story behind the lights began in 1986 when the famous Lucy Spata began decorating her home with extravagant Christmas lights and glowing angel figurines in honor of her late mother.

She initially faced criticism from neighbors regarding the bright display at night and commotion with many telling her to move. However, her resistance to cave in eventually resulted in a neighborhood embracing the holiday spirit, creating the famous seasonal hotspot.

Today, the lights in Dyker Heights stretch from 11th and 13th Avenue and 83rd to 86th Street, with many homes participating in the festive tradition with some spending up $100,000!
